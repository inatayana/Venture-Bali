# Venture Bali - Refund & Cancellation Policy

> **Version:** 1.0.0 | **Updated:** August 27, 2026
> **Applies to:** All bookings through Venture Bali platform

## 1. Overview

This policy balances customer protection with operational needs. Key principles:
- **Transparency:** Clear, documented policy communicated upfront
- **Fairness:** Differentiated treatment based on timing
- **Protection:** Fallback mechanisms for emergencies
- **Simplicity:** Easy to understand and apply

## 2. Refund Policy Timeline

### Standard Cancellation Windows

| Time Before Activity | Refund Rate | Processing Time |
|---------------------|-------------|----------------|
| ≥ 72 hours (3 days) | **100% refund** | 1-3 business days |
| 48-72 hours | **70% refund** | 1-3 business days |
| 24-48 hours | **30% refund** | 1-3 business days |
| < 24 hours or No-show | **0% refund** | N/A |

**Key definitions:**
- 72 hours = 3x 24-hour periods before activity start time
- Activity start time = scheduled time on booking voucher
- Business days = Mon-Fri (excluding Indonesian holidays)

## 3. Provider/Force Majeure Cancellations

| Scenario | Refund Rate | Alternative Action |
|----------|-------------|-------------------|
| Natural disaster/weather | **100% refund** | Reschedule to new date |
| Government restrictions | **100% refund** | Reschedule to new date |
| Provider insolvency | **100% refund** | Reassign to alternative provider |
| Safety concerns | **100% refund** | Reassign to alternative activity |

## 4. No-Show Policy

### A. Definition
**No-show:** Customer fails to appear without prior cancellation.

### B. Consequences
1. **No refund:** 0% refund for no-show bookings
2. **Account status:** Repeated no-shows may result in account suspension
3. **Provider payment:** Provider still receives full payment

### C. Prevention Measures
1. **24-hour reminder:** WhatsApp notification before activity
2. **12-hour reminder:** Email notification before activity
3. **1-hour reminder:** Final confirmation reminder

## 5. Special Cases

### A. Medical Reasons
- **Customer:** Must provide medical certificate within 48 hours of cancellation
- **Provider staff:** Full refund with no questions asked

### B. Insurance Coverage
- **Venture Bali insurance partner:** Covers up to 80% of non-refundable bookings
- **Customer's own travel insurance:** Varies by policy
- **No insurance:** Standard policy applies

### C. Multiple Participants
- **Full group cancellation:** Full refund if all participants cancel together
- **Partial cancellations:** No partial refunds (book as separate bookings)

### D. Payment Method Issues
- **Failed refunds:** If refund fails due to payment method issues, customer must contact support
- **Processing delays:** Refunds may take additional 5-7 business days

## 6. Refund Processing

### A. Trigger Events
Refunds are processed when:
1. Customer submits cancellation via app/website
2. Support team processes manual cancellation
3. Force majeure event is confirmed
4. Insurance claim is approved

### B. Processing Steps
1. **Verification:** Check booking status, cancellation timing, required documentation
2. **Calculation:** Apply appropriate refund rate
3. **Approval:** Manager review for cancellations >72 hours
4. **Processing:** Transfer funds to original payment method
5. **Notification:** Send refund confirmation to customer

### C. Timeline
| Refund Type | Processing Time | Notification |
|-------------|----------------|--------------|
| Standard customer cancellation | 1-3 business days | Email immediately after processing |
| Force majeure cancellation | 1-3 business days | Email immediately after processing |
| Insurance processing | 5-10 business days | Email after approval |
| Medical exceptions | 3-5 business days | Email after verification |

## 7. Dispute Resolution

### A. Escalation Process
1. **Customer portal:** Submit cancellation request via app
2. **Support ticket:** For complex cases or manual cancellations
3. **Manager review:** For disputes over refund amounts
4. **Final decision:** Customer service manager

### B. Timeline
- Initial response: Within 24 hours
- Final decision: Within 5 business days
- Refund processing: As per standard timeline

### C. Appeals
Customers may appeal refund decisions within:
- **7 days:** For standard cancellations
- **14 days:** For force majeure or medical reasons

## 8. Data & Documentation

### A. Required Documentation
| Scenario | Required Documents |
|----------|-------------------|
| Medical cancellation | Medical certificate, doctor's note |
| Force majeure | Official reports, government notices |
| Provider issues | Provider cancellation notice, proof |
| Insurance claims | Insurance policy, claim documents |

### B. Record Retention
- **Customer cancellations:** 3 years
- **Refund records:** 5 years (tax compliance)
- **Dispute documentation:** 7 years
- **Force majeure records:** 10 years

## 9. System Integration

### A. Booking System
The refund policy is integrated into:
1. **Booking flow:** Display policy during checkout
2. **Cancellation interface:** Clear timeline and rates
3. **Refund calculator:** Real-time refund estimates
4. **Automated processing:** Without manual intervention

### B. Provider Portal
Providers can:
1. **View cancellations:** See cancellation history and reasons
2. **Understand impact:** See how cancellations affect their revenue
3. **Provide feedback:** Report systemic issues affecting cancellations

## 10. Monitoring & Reporting

### A. Key Metrics
- **Cancellation rate:** % of bookings cancelled
- **No-show rate:** % of bookings not attended
- **Refund processing time:** Average days to process
- **Customer satisfaction:** Cancellation policy ratings
- **Provider impact:** Revenue impact analysis

### B. Monthly Reports
1. **Cancellation trends:** By time window, day of week
2. **Force majeure events:** Frequency and impact
3. **No-show analysis:** Patterns and prevention effectiveness
4. **Insurance claims:** Volume and processing efficiency
5. **Compliance audit:** Policy adherence and improvements

## 11. Policy Updates

### A. Review Schedule
- **Quarterly review:** For operational and customer feedback
- **Annual audit:** Full policy review and updates
- **Emergency update:** Immediate updates for significant changes

### B. Communication
- **Policy changes:** 30 days notice for customer-facing updates
- **System updates:** Documentation updates in code comments
- **Provider notification:** 14 days notice for provider-facing changes

## 12. Emergency Procedures

### A. Immediate Refunds
For life-threatening emergencies or natural disasters:
1. **Customer safety:** Immediate full refund without documentation
2. **Emergency contact:** Verified through customer support team
3. **Follow-up:** Documentation required within 72 hours

### B. System Failures
If refund processing systems fail:
1. **Manual processing:** Support team processes refunds individually
2. **Timeline extension:** Processing may take up to 15 business days
3. **Compensation:** 1% daily interest for delays beyond promised timeframe

## 13. Special Provisions

### A. Indonesian Customer Protection Law
All refunds comply with Indonesian consumer protection laws:
- **Right to cancel:** Within 7 days for certain products/services
- **Transparency:** Clear information about cancellation terms
- **Fair treatment:** Non-discriminatory refund policies

### B. International Regulations
For international customers:
- **Currency conversion:** Refunds processed in original currency
- **Cross-border fees:** Customer responsible for bank fees
- **Regulatory compliance:** Adherence to both Indonesian and international laws

## 14. Training & Documentation

### A. Staff Training
All customer-facing staff must:
1. **Understand policy:** Complete training on all scenarios
2. **Apply consistently:** Follow standardized procedures
3. **Communicate clearly:** Explain policy in customer-friendly language
4. **Handle disputes:** Follow escalation procedures

### B. Customer Education
Customers receive:
1. **Policy display:** Clear presentation during booking
2. **Confirmation:** Policy confirmation in booking confirmation
3. **Reminders:** 24-hour reminder including cancellation policy
4. **Support materials:** FAQ and contact information

## 15. Conclusion

The Venture Bali Refund & Cancellation Policy balances:
- **Customer protection:** Fair compensation for cancellations
- **Operational efficiency:** Streamlined processing
- **Risk management:** Financial protection for the platform
- **Compliance:** Adherence to all relevant laws and regulations

This policy is designed to be customer-friendly while ensuring the long-term sustainability of the Venture Bali platform.
