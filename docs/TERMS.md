## 1. About these terms
BaliWild is operated by PT Natayana Bali Tours — a single-brand operator of adventure activities in Bali, Indonesia. We manage the complete booking journey; the activity is performed by licensed local providers appointed by BaliWild.

By confirming a booking you agree to these terms, the cancellation policy and the activity safety waiver.

## 2. Our role and the provider's role
**BaliWild is responsible for:** (a) the reservation and slot hold; (b) payment processing; (c) the booking reference and e-voucher; (d) pickup and meeting-point coordination; (e) reschedule, cancellation and refund handling; (f) first-line support before/during/after the trip; (g) relaying booking details to the appointed provider.

**The appointed provider is responsible for:** (a) performing the activity; (b) safety equipment, guides and on-site safety management; (c) activity insurance and the facilities described on the package page; (d) check-in safety briefing and rules; (e) services explicitly included in the package (e.g. entrance fees, lunch, lockers/showers where specified).

Guests never need to contact the provider — BaliWild is the single point of contact and coordinates with providers on the guest's behalf. Provider identities and commercial arrangements are confidential and never disclosed to guests.

## 3. Bookings, holds and confirmation
- Bookings confirm when payment is settled and the booking reference + e-voucher are issued.
- The lead guest is responsible for accurate names, contact details, participant count and pickup information.
- A 10-minute hold protects the slot while paying. If the payment session expires, the slot is released.
- Booking details are relayed to the appointed provider for arrival, safety registration and logistics.

## 4. Prices and payment
- All prices are in IDR and include the services listed on the package page.
- Payments are processed by Midtrans (QRIS, virtual accounts, e-wallets, cards). Card data is never stored.
- A promotional price and a coupon can never be combined. The checkout amount is the charged amount.
- Pickup surcharges (outer/custom-quote areas) are shown and agreed at checkout.

## 5. Cancellation, reschedule and no-show
| When | Outcome |
|---|---|
| ≥ 24 hours before the activity | 20% cancellation fee → 80% refund |
| < 24 hours before the activity | Cannot cancel |
| Reschedule ≥ 24 hours before | Free, subject to availability |
| < 24 hours before | Cannot reschedule |
| No-show without confirming ≥ 1 day before | 100% fee, no refund |

Approved refunds return to the original payment method within 3 business days. See `docs/REFUND_POLICY.md` (v2.0.0) and the public `/refund` page for the policy and worked examples.

## 6. Changes by us or by the weather
If an activity cannot run safely, the guest is offered another date or a full refund. Departure times, meeting points or routes may change for safety reasons, without reducing the experience's value.

## 7. Guest responsibilities
- Meet the published age, weight and health requirements.
- Sign the digital safety waiver at booking and complete any safety declaration at check-in; registration at the meeting point includes identity and safety checks.
- Arrive at least 15 minutes before departure with valid ID and package inclusions.
- Follow the safety briefing and the guide's instructions. No alcohol or drugs before or during the activity.

## 8. Pickup and meeting point
- Self-drive packages start at the meeting point on the package page.
- Transfer packages include hotel pickup; the pickup area may carry a surcharge shown at checkout.
- A "TBA" pickup address must be confirmed no later than H-1. Outer areas require a custom quote.

## 9. Liability and insurance
- Adventure activities carry inherent risk. Participation requires the safety waiver, accepted at booking and re-confirmed at check-in.
- The appointed provider carries activity insurance (equipment, guides, facilities) as described on the package page. Guests are encouraged to hold personal travel insurance for medical care, evacuation and belongings.
- BaliWild's liability is limited to its obligations as coordinator of the booking process (reservation, payment, voucher, coordination, refunds). BaliWild is not the operator and is not liable for loss/injury/damage arising from inherent activity risks, except where Indonesian law prohibits such exclusion.
- Nothing limits liability for gross negligence or wilful misconduct. On any incident, BaliWild actively coordinates the response and resolution with the provider, including insurance-claims assistance.

## 10. Your data
We use name, email, WhatsApp number, billing details and booking information to process the booking, issue the e-voucher, coordinate pickup and share the manifest with the appointed provider. Only what the provider needs is shared. Provider names/structures are never published. Data retention follows statutory and safety-audit needs.

## 11. Dispute resolution and contact
These terms are governed by Indonesian law; disputes are settled in the courts of Bali, Indonesia. Contact us first: support@baliwild.com (existing bookings) or hello@baliwild.com.

## 12. Changes to these terms
We may update these terms. The version shown on the page, and the version accepted at booking, applies. Material changes are announced on this page.
