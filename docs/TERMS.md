## 1. About these terms
BaliWild is a booking platform for adventure activities in Bali, Indonesia. We arrange the reservation, payment and customer support; the activity itself is delivered by a licensed local activity partner.

By confirming a booking you agree to these terms, the cancellation policy and the activity safety waiver.

## 2. Bookings and confirmation
- Bookings are confirmed when payment is settled and a booking reference with an e-voucher is issued.
- The lead guest is responsible for the accuracy of names, contact details, participant count and pickup information.
- A 10-minute hold protects the slot while paying. If the payment session expires, the slot is released and must be re-booked.

## 3. Prices and payment
- All prices are in IDR and include the services listed on the package.
- Payments are processed by Midtrans (QRIS, virtual accounts, e-wallets, cards). Card data is never stored.
- A promotional price and a coupon can never be combined. The amount shown at checkout is the amount charged.

## 4. Cancellation, reschedule and no-show
| When | Outcome |
|---|---|
| ≥ 24 hours before the activity | 20% cancellation fee → 80% refund |
| < 24 hours before the activity | Cannot cancel |
| Reschedule ≥ 24 hours before | Free |
| No-show without confirming ≥ 1 day before | 100% fee, no refund |

Approved refunds return to the original payment method within 3 business days.

## 5. Changes by the operator or weather
If an activity cannot run safely, the guest is offered another date or a full refund. Departure times or routes may change for safety reasons.

## 6. Guest responsibilities
- Meet the published age, weight and health requirements.
- Arrive at least 15 minutes before departure with valid ID and the items listed under "What to bring".
- Follow the safety briefing and the guide's instructions. No alcohol or drugs before/during the activity.

## 7. Pickup and meeting point
- Self-drive packages start at the meeting point on the product page.
- Transfer packages include hotel pickup; the pickup area may carry a surcharge shown at checkout.
- A "TBA" pickup address must be confirmed no later than H-1. Outer areas require a custom quote.

## 8. Liability and insurance
Adventure activities carry inherent risk. Participation requires accepting the safety waiver. Basic activity insurance is included as listed on the package; personal travel insurance is recommended.

## 9. Your data
We use name, email, WhatsApp, billing details and booking information to process the booking, issue the e-voucher, coordinate pickup and maintain the activity manifest. Only information required to deliver the activity is shared with the activity partner; partner names are never published.

## 10. Governing law and contact
Governed by Indonesian law; disputes in the courts of Bali, Indonesia. Questions: email `hello@baliwild.com` for general enquiries or `support@baliwild.com` for an existing booking, reschedule, refund or on-trip emergency.
