## RECITALS

A. Platform operates **BaliWild Adventures** (www.baliwild.com), an online travel platform for adventure tourism in Bali.
B. Operator is a licensed adventure activity operator in Bali with valid permits, licenses, and insurance.
C. Parties wish to establish a partnership where Platform markets, sells, and coordinates bookings for Operator's activities, while Operator executes the activities.
D. Platform acts as **Merchant of Record** and **Marketing/Booking/Hosting Partner**; Operator acts as **licensed Activity Operator**.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS & INTERPRETATION

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Activity** | Adventure experience(s) listed in Schedule A (e.g., ATV, Rafting, Tubing, Trekking, Canyoning, Cycling, Water Sports, Combo). |
| **Booking** | Confirmed reservation made by Guest via Platform, evidenced by BaliWild Voucher. |
| **Guest** | End customer who purchases and participates in an Activity. |
| **Voucher** | Digital document issued by Platform confirming Booking, containing QR code, Guest details, Activity, date, time, pickup info. |
| **Net Rate** | Price per Guest paid by Platform to Operator (exclusive of Platform commission). |
| **Retail Price** | Price per Guest displayed and charged by Platform to Guest. |
| **Commission** | Retail Price minus Net Rate. |
| **Business Day** | **Monday–Saturday, 09:00–17:00 WITA**. Bookings run **24/7**. Activities run **24/7 except Hari Raya Nyepi**. Indonesian public holidays are not Business Days. |
| **Gross Negligence** | Conscious and voluntary disregard of the need to use reasonable care, likely to cause foreseeable grave injury or harm. |
| **Force Majeure** | Events beyond reasonable control: natural disasters, extreme weather (BMKG warning), government actions, pandemics, terrorism. |

## 2. SCOPE & APPOINTMENT

2.1 **Appointment.** Platform appoints Operator as non-exclusive provider of Activities listed in **Schedule A**. Operator accepts appointment.

2.2 **Non-Exclusivity.** Platform may appoint other Operators for same or similar Activities. Operator may work with other platforms.

2.3 **Independent Contractor.** Operator is an **independent contractor**. Nothing in this Agreement creates employment, agency, joint venture, or partnership. Operator has no authority to bind Platform.

2.4 **Merchant of Record.** Platform is **Merchant of Record** — Guest pays Platform; Platform pays Operator Net Rate. Guest contract is with Platform.

## 7. SAFETY STANDARDS & OPERATIONAL REQUIREMENTS (SCHEDULE C)

| Requirement | Minimum Standard |
|-------------|------------------|
| **Guide Certification** | IRF (Rafting), UIAA/IFMGA (Trekking/Canyoning), ATV Safety Cert (ATV), PADI (Diving) |
| **Guide:Guest Ratio** | Rafting 1:6, ATV 1:4, Tubing 1:8, Trekking 1:8, Canyoning 1:4 |
| **Safety Gear** | UIAA/CE certified; monthly inspection log; replacement per manufacturer schedule |
| **Emergency SOP** | Written protocol: evacuation, medical, weather, missing person, equipment failure |
| **Drill Frequency** | Quarterly full emergency drill; documented |
| **Risk Assessment** | Per Activity, updated annually or after incident |
| **Weather Protocol** | Real-time monitoring; guide authority to cancel/modify |
| **Guest Screening** | Health declaration, weight/height check (ATV), swim ability (water activities) |
| **Guide Association & Training** | **All guides must be registered with a legitimate organizer association; have completed rigorous field training per on-site competency and obtained association and organizer approval.** |
| **Rafting — Capacity Per Boat** | **Standard: max 6 persons per boat (INCLUDING 1 guide). Minimum 2 guests per booking. Private boat rafting: exempt from 6-person limit, capacity per vendor specs/river regulations.** |
| **Rafting — Private Boat** | Bookable separately; price & capacity per Schedule A private variant. |

## 10. PRICING, COMMISSION & PAYMENT (SCHEDULE A)

10.1 **Retail Price** set by Platform. **Net Rate** agreed per Schedule A per variant.

10.2 **Commission** = Retail Price – Net Rate. Platform retains Commission.

10.3 **Payment Terms.** Platform pays Net Rate **net 14 Business Days** after Activity completion (reconciliation by 5th Business Day of following month).

10.3 **No-Show / Guest Cancel <24h.** No payment to Operator. Platform retains full Retail Price (per Refund Policy Schedule B).

10.4 **Currency.** All amounts **IDR**. Net Rate fixed for Term unless mutual written amendment.

10.4 **Annual Indexation.** Optional annual CPI adjustment (BI core inflation) with 60-day notice. Cap: 5% per year.

10.5 **Invoices.** Operator issues tax-compliant invoice (e-Faktur) monthly by 5th Business Day.

10.6 **Payment Disputes.** Undisputed amounts paid on time; disputed amounts held in escrow max 30 days, resolved per Clause 16.

## 11. CANCELLATION, REFUND & FORCE MAJEURE (SCHEDULE B)

| Scenario | Guest Refund | Operator Payment |
|----------|--------------|------------------|
| **Guest Cancel ≥24h** | 80% (20% fee) | No Net Rate |
| **Guest Cancel <24h / No-Show** | 0% | No Net Rate |
| **Platform Cancel (Capacity/Weather)** | 100% or Reschedule | No Net Rate |
| **Operator Cancel (Non-Force Majeure)** | 100% + Platform may claim damages | No Net Rate |
| **Force Majeure (Extreme Weather/Disaster/Govt)** | 100% or Reschedule (Guest choice) | No Net Rate for cancelled; completed Activities paid |
| **Partial Completion** | Pro-rata per Platform policy | Pro-rata Net Rate |

**Force Majeure:** Extreme weather (BMKG warning), natural disaster, government order, pandemic, terrorism. Affected Party notifies within 24h. Mitigation required.

**Sunk Cost Deduction (Force Majeure <48h):** Operator may deduct documented sunk costs (max 20% Net Rate) with supporting evidence.

## 15. INDEMNIFICATION & LIABILITY

15.1 **Operator Indemnifies Platform** against all claims, damages, losses, costs (including legal fees) arising from:
- Operator negligence, gross negligence, wilful misconduct
- Guest injury/death/property damage during Activity
- Operator breach of licenses, permits, insurance, safety standards
- Third-party claims (guests, authorities, other partners)

15.2 **Platform Indemnifies Operator** against claims arising from:
- Platform negligence in marketing, payment, refund, data processing
- Platform breach of DPA, payment terms, brand usage
- Guest claims solely due to Platform platform error (double booking, wrong voucher)

15.3 **Liability Cap.** Each Party's aggregate liability capped at **total Net Rate paid/payable in preceding 12 months**, **EXCEPT:**
- Gross negligence / wilful misconduct
- **Death / permanent disability / serious bodily injury — UNLIMITED per Indonesian law (KUHPerdata Art. 1365, UU 8/1999)**
- Fraud / fraudulent misrepresentation
- IP infringement
- Breach of confidentiality / data privacy (UU 27/2022)
- Indemnification obligations
- Breach of safety standards (Clause 7)

15.4 **No Consequential Damages.** Neither Party liable for lost profits, revenue, data, reputation, opportunity (except carve-outs above).

15.4 **Guest Property Liability.** Operator liable for Guest property (phones, cameras, etc.) **only if loss due to Operator negligence** (max IDR 5 Million per Guest). Guests advised not to bring valuables.
