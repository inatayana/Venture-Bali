# Venture Bali — Activity Safety Waiver / Release of Liability (DRAFT v1)

> **Status: DRAFT — pending legal review before launch.**
> Operational copy lives in `src/lib/legal.ts` (rendered at `/waiver`).
> Version: 2026-11-01
>
> **Why this matters:** this waiver is accepted with a mandatory checkbox at checkout and the accepted version + timestamp are stored on the booking (`waiverAcceptedAt`, `waiverVersion`). It is the legal basis for the insurance manifest.

## 1. Acknowledgment of risk
I understand that adventure activities in Bali — including ATV riding, rafting, tubing, cycling, trekking, canyoning and water sports — involve inherent risks: injury, illness, equipment failure, changing weather, river conditions and incidents during transport. I participate voluntarily and accept those risks for myself and for any minors I am responsible for.

## 2. Medical declaration
I confirm I am physically able to participate and have disclosed any condition that could affect my safety, including pregnancy, heart conditions, epilepsy, recent surgery, back or neck problems, and asthma. The guide may exclude me if participation would be unsafe; the cancellation policy then applies.

## 3. Safety rules and equipment
I will wear the provided safety equipment, follow the safety briefing and follow the instructions of guides and safety staff at all times. I will not be under the influence of alcohol or drugs, nor operate equipment in a way that endangers others.

## 4. Release of liability
To the fullest extent permitted by Indonesian law, I release the activity operator, its guides and staff, and Venture Bali from liability for loss, injury or damage arising from the ordinary risks of the activity. This release does not cover gross negligence or wilful misconduct and does not remove rights that Indonesian law does not allow to be excluded.

## 5. Minors
Participants under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian, who accepts this waiver on their behalf and supervises them during the activity.

## 6. Medical assistance and images
I authorise the operator to arrange emergency medical assistance if needed, at my cost where not covered by the included insurance. The operator may photograph or film the activity for documentation and safety purposes.

## 7. Acknowledgment
I have read and understood this waiver, had the opportunity to ask questions, and accept it voluntarily. My acceptance is recorded with a timestamp and the waiver version.
